What Studio is

Studio is software you run on your own computer. It cuts long videos into short vertical clips, checks them, and publishes the clips you approve to accounts you connect, through the official APIs of TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Studio is not a hosted service. I do not host, receive or review your videos, accounts or data. How Studio handles data is described in the privacy policy.

Disconnecting an account in Studio deletes its stored tokens and asks the platform to revoke Studio’s access. You can also remove Studio’s access in each platform’s settings at any time.

Approval and scheduling
