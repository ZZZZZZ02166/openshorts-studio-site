OpenShortsStudio

A desktop app that cuts long videos into vertical Shorts, then posts each to TikTok, YouTube or Instagram once you approve it.

Transcription and moment-picking run with local models by default: Whisper for speech and a language model served by Ollama, so your footage is not sent to an AI service.

Captions follow the look of your own channel. Studio measures caption size, position and case in Shorts you have already published, then keeps captions clear of faces where the frame allows.

Common questions

Does the developer see my TikTok account?

No. Studio has no server and no sign-up. It talks to TikTok directly from your computer and keeps the tokens there, encrypted. Nothing reaches me unless you email me.

Can Studio post to TikTok without me?

No. Every TikTok post needs your approval of that exact export, a visibility you choose yourself, and your agreement to TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation. Studio’s optional automatic approval covers only YouTube and Instagram.

Which TikTok permissions does it ask for?

user.info.basic to show which account is connected, video.publish to post a video you approved, and video.upload to send a video to your TikTok drafts instead. Studio requests only the ones enabled for its TikTok app.

Will my TikTok posts be public?

Only if TikTok allows it. Studio offers the visibility options TikTok returns for your account. Until TikTok has audited an app, TikTok keeps that app’s posts private.

How do I disconnect and delete everything?

Disconnect TikTok in Studio’s Local setup: Studio revokes its access and deletes the tokens. You can also remove the app in TikTok’s settings. The data deletion page lists every step.

Is Studio made by TikTok?

No. OpenShorts Studio is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube or Instagram. It is built on OpenShorts, open-source software released under the MIT licence.
