Delete your data

Studio keeps its data on your own computer, so you can delete all of it yourself. Here is every step.

Last updated 15 November 2026

  2. Remove access in TikTok
  3. Delete local data
  4. Posts on TikTok
  5. YouTube and Instagram
  6. Ask me

  1. Disconnect TikTok in Studio

    In Studio, open Local setup → Connected accounts → TikTok and choose Disconnect. Studio asks TikTok to revoke its access and immediately deletes the access and refresh tokens from your computer.

    If Studio then says “Local account disconnected. Remove this app in the platform’s connected-app settings too.”, TikTok did not confirm the revocation. Do the next step as well.

  2. Remove Studio’s access in TikTok

    In the TikTok app, tap Profile, then the Menu button, then Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions. Choose OpenShorts Studio and tap Remove access. TikTok describes these steps in its help article Connect to third-party apps.

  3. Delete Studio’s data from your computer

    Quit Studio, then delete its data folder: studio-data inside Studio’s folder, or the folder set by STUDIO_DATA_DIR. This removes:

    • the database, with your account details, encrypted tokens and post history;
    • the encryption key;
    • your projects, transcripts, clips and exports.

    To remove your TikTok developer app keys as well, delete the STUDIO_TIKTOK_CLIENT_ID and STUDIO_TIKTOK_CLIENT_SECRET lines from Studio’s .env file. Copies in your own backups are deleted when you delete those backups.

  4. Delete posts on TikTok

    Studio cannot delete videos that are already on TikTok. Delete them in the TikTok app. TikTok handles your account and posts under its Privacy Policy.

  5. YouTube and Instagram

    Disconnect them in Studio the same way. You can also remove Studio’s access in your Google account permissions and in Instagram’s settings for connected apps and websites.

  6. Ask me

    I don’t hold a copy of your Studio data, so there is nothing of it on my side to delete. If you emailed me and want our correspondence deleted, or you want help with any step, write to clipwave1104@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. I’ll confirm by email once it is done.
