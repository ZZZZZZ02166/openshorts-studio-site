Email is the way to reach me. I read every message and reply by email.

What to write about

  • Questions about Studio and how it works.
  • Privacy and data requests: use the subject “Privacy request” or “Data deletion”. The data deletion page covers what you can do yourself straight away.
  • Security problems: use the subject “Security”, and please don’t publish details until the problem is fixed.

Problems with your TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account itself are handled by those platforms’ own support teams. I can’t access or change your account on any platform.

Keep secrets out of email

Never send passwords, access tokens, client secrets, the contents of your .env file or private videos by email. I will never ask for them.
